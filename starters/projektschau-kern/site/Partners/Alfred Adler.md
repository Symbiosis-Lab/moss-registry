byline: Physician and psychotherapist · 1870–1937
---

Founded individual psychology in Vienna in 1907. In 1920 he became director of the first clinic for child psychology in Vienna and until 1929 lectured at the City of Vienna's Pedagogical Institute; within the school reform he and his colleagues opened about thirty child guidance centres. He moved to the United States in 1934.
