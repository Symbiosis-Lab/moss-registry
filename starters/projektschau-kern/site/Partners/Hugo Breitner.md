![Hugo Breitner (1873–1946), politician. Theo Bauer, 1928. Wien Museum, inv. 49172, CC0.|align-right 30%](Hugo%20Breitner.jpg)

Banker and Social Democrat, a member of the city council from 1918 to 1933 and, from 1 June 1920, executive city councillor for finance. In 1923 he introduced a system of city taxes whose rates rose steeply with the sum taxed; the best known, the housing construction tax of 1 February 1923, was the basis of the city's housing programme. He resigned in autumn 1932 and died in Claremont, California, in 1946.
