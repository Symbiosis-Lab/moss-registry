Teacher and Social Democratic politician. From April 1919 to October 1920 he was the republic's first under-secretary of state for education; from 1922 to 1934 he was president of the Vienna City School Board, and the Vienna school reform was carried out under his leadership. He was arrested in his office in February 1934 and taken to the Wöllersdorf detention camp; he died in July 1935.
