A pupil of Otto Wagner, he worked for the Vienna city building office from 1909, in leading positions from 1921. As a city building officer from 1924 he created several large housing blocks of Red Vienna and is the foremost representative of its municipal housing style; his main work is the Karl-Marx-Hof in Döbling.
