title: Otto Neurath
partners_page: true
byline: Economist and museum founder · 1882–1945
---

Founded the Austrian Association for Settlement and Allotment Gardens in 1920 and was its general secretary; founded the Museum for Settlement and Town Planning (1923 or 1924, the sources differ), from which the Society and Economy Museum grew in January 1925. With its team he developed the Vienna Method of Pictorial Statistics, renamed Isotype in 1934. After arrest attempts in 1934 and a fire that destroyed the museum's exhibits, he emigrated to The Hague with four of its staff. He died in Oxford in 1945.
