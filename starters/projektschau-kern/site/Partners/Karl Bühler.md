Psychologist, professor of psychology at the University of Vienna and head of its Psychological Institute from 1922. The institute was founded through the city's efforts to reorganise the school system on new scientific knowledge of child development. He was briefly detained by the National Socialists in March 1938 and emigrated in 1940 by way of Oslo and London to the United States.
