Worked for the Vienna city building office from 1911, as senior building commissioner from 1920 and chief city building officer from 1929. His focus was buildings for child welfare such as schools and kindergartens, notably the children's reception centre at Ayrenhoffgasse 9; he also planned many municipal housing estates.
