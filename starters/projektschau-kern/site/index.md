# The New Vienna, 1919–1934

Between 1919 and 1934 the City of Vienna built flats, baths, clinics and schools, and from 1926 published the whole programme as one overview, *Das Neue Wien*. This board rebuilds that overview from today's archives: every project, its facts and where each fact comes from.
