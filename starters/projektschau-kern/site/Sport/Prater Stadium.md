The city's stadium, decided in 1928 for the Workers' Olympiad of 1931 and opened in time for it.

![The Prater Stadium in Vienna. Martin Gerlach jun., around 1931. Wien Museum, inv. 188325, CC0.](Prater%20Stadium.jpg)

Opened 11 July 1931.
