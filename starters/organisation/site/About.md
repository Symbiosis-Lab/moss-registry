- What: the Chautauqua Literary and Scientific Circle (C. L. S. C.), founded 1878 at Chautauqua, New York: a four-year course of home reading, kept by local circles, memorial days and a yearly Recognition Day
- Sources: *The Chautauquan*, Meadville, Pa., and Chautauqua, N.Y.: July 1884 (Project Gutenberg #55208), October 1884 (#55218), March 1885 (#55060), April 1885 (#55061), May 1885 (#55331); John H. Vincent, *The Chautauqua Movement* (Boston: Chautauqua Press, 1886)
- Voice: the unsigned “C. L. S. C.” department of *The Chautauquan*; anything by John H. Vincent carries his name
- Images: Detroit Publishing Co. photochroms, c. 1898, Library of Congress, no known restrictions on publication (2016794165, 2016794163, 2016794169, 2016794164, 2016797619); Golden Gate for Graduates of the CLSC, postcard no. 81470, New York Public Library scan on Wikimedia Commons, public domain
- Places: coordinates from Wikidata
- Public domain: United States publications of 1882 to 1898, all before 1931
- Not used: the materials of the present Chautauqua Institution
