# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Long pages can go to LinkedIn as articles. Turn on Articles in the channel's settings. LinkedIn's API cannot make articles, so moss shows the article with a button that copies it and opens LinkedIn's article editor in the panel; paste, check and publish there, then list the article's address under `syndicated:`. Posts that fit are still posted by moss, and with Articles off a long page is still posted as its opening with a link.
- A copy listed by its address under `syndicated:` is edited and removed on LinkedIn itself: moss says so.
- Needs moss 0.15.25.

## 0.1.1

- The channel button shows the LinkedIn logo.

## 0.1.0

- A preview (`"preview": true`): moss offers and installs it only for users who have turned on Preview features in its Settings.
- Posts each new post's copy to your LinkedIn profile through moss's syndication contract: posts that fit whole, longer posts and articles as their opening with an article card linking the original.
- Edits a copy's text in place when its post changes, and removes a copy when its post is deleted.
