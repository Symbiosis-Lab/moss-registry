# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Works without API keys: X's composer opens in moss's panel with the copy filled in, and you press Post. A thread's later parts are shown to add as replies. List the post's address under `syndicated:` to keep track of it. With API keys, moss still posts by itself.

## 0.1.1

- The channel button shows the X logo.

## 0.1.0

- A preview (`"preview": true`): moss offers and installs it only for users who have turned on Preview features in its Settings.
- Posts each new post's copy to X through moss's syndication contract: short posts whole, posts written in parts as threads of replies, longer posts and articles as their opening with a link to the original, which X shows as a card.
- Looks an interrupted send up among the account's latest posts before posting again, and removes a copy when its post is deleted.
