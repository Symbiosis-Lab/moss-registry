# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- A preview (`"preview": true`): moss offers and installs it only for users who have turned on Preview features in its Settings.
- Hands each article to Substack's own editor: moss shows the article with a button that copies it and opens your publication's editor in the panel; you paste and publish.
- Finds the published post in your publication's feed by its link back to the page, and records its address.
